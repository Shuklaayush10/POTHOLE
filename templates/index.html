How It Works

A simple, powerful pipeline to process road hazards from capture to repair recommendation.

1. Capture

Take a photo of the road hazard and upload it to the platform.

2. Analyze

OpenCV algorithms detect the pothole and compute dimensions.

3. Evaluate

Severity is calculated based on area and depth proxy.

4. Recommend

Actionable repair steps are generated for road authorities.
